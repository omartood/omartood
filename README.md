## Omar Tood

Machine learning engineer and AI researcher at [Somast](https://www.somast.so).
I work on large language models, agentic systems, and natural language
processing for Somali.

Somali is spoken by more than 20 million people and holds an almost invisible
share of the data that trains modern AI. Most of what I build is aimed at
closing that gap: open datasets, Somali-native models, and the tooling and
teaching that let other people build on top of them.

More at [omartood.com](https://www.omartood.com).
